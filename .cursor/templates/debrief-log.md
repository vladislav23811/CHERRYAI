# Debrief Log Template

Use this template at the end of a milestone or significant session to document what was achieved, what risks remain, and what the next steps are. This helps maintain context across different AGI sessions.

**Instructions:** Copy the block below and paste it into Cursor Composer or Chat, asking the AGI to fill it out based on the session's work.

---

```text
Please generate a Milestone Debrief Log using the following format based on the work we just completed:

## Milestone Debrief: <Milestone Name>
**Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>

### 🎯 Delivered
- <Bullet points of what now works or was completed>

### 📁 Files Changed
- `<path/to/file1>`: <Brief reason for change>
- `<path/to/file2>`: <Brief reason for change>

### ✅ Validation Performed
- <What tests, linting, or manual checks were run to verify the changes?>

### ⚠️ Risks & Assumptions
- <Any known issues, technical debt introduced, or assumptions made>

### ⏭️ Next Actions
1. <Best next step>
2. <Second best next step>
```
